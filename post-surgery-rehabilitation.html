What Is Post-Surgery Rehabilitation?

Recovering after orthopedic surgery — including Total Knee Replacement (TKR), Total Hip Replacement (THR), ligament reconstruction, or spinal surgery — requires proper rehabilitation to regain strength, flexibility, and confidence. Many patients experience pain, swelling, stiffness, and difficulty walking after surgery. Early physiotherapy helps improve recovery, restore independence, and achieve the best possible surgical outcome.

At Renoxa Physiotherapy, we provide expert home physiotherapy for post-surgery rehabilitation across Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Gurugram and the wider Delhi NCR region. Our treatment focuses on reducing pain, restoring mobility, and building long-term strength — with chiropractic care available where appropriate to improve spinal movement and reduce stiffness.

Common Symptoms

Pain and swelling around the surgical site Stiffness and reduced range of motion Muscle weakness from reduced activity Difficulty walking or climbing stairs Reduced confidence bearing weight on the operated limb Scar tissue restricting movement

Frequently Asked Questions

When should physiotherapy start after knee or hip replacement?
Timing depends on your surgeon's protocol — many programs begin gentle movement within a day or two of surgery. We coordinate directly with your surgical team's guidance.
How long does recovery take after a total knee or hip replacement?
Most patients see significant improvement in mobility and strength within 6–12 weeks, though full recovery and muscle strength can continue improving for several months.
Are there movements I need to avoid after hip replacement?
Yes, most surgeons give specific 'hip precautions' in the early weeks — such as avoiding certain hip angles or crossing the legs — which we build into your rehabilitation plan.
Can post-surgery rehabilitation be done at home?
Yes, we provide home physiotherapy after knee and hip replacement across Delhi, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad and Gurugram, which is often easier in the early recovery period before travel is comfortable.
